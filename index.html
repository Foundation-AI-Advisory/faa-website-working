Executive answers

A few practical answers.

Business outcomes first.
Data, workflows, then applied AI.

What is Foundation AI Advisory?

Foundation AI Advisory is a business-first AI advisory firm for mid-market operators. Foundation AI Advisory fixes the data, workflow, and system foundation before applying AI.

What is Foundation AI Advisory’s AI implementation methodology?

Foundation AI Advisory follows three steps in order: Data Curation & Governance, Workflow Optimization, and AI Design & Implementation.

Why does Foundation AI Advisory start with data and workflows before AI?

AI depends on the system underneath it. If the data is incomplete, the workflow is undefined, or the owner of the output is unclear, AI will amplify those problems instead of solving them.

Who does Foundation AI Advisory serve?

Foundation AI Advisory serves CEOs, Presidents, CFOs, Controllers, CIOs, and IT leaders in mid-market companies across industrial, construction, logistics, financial services, professional services, AI, data, and IT systems environments.

What outcomes should AI produce?

AI should improve margin, throughput, cycle time, cash flow, risk exposure, or operational visibility. Foundation AI Advisory does not recommend AI work without a clear business outcome and path to production.
