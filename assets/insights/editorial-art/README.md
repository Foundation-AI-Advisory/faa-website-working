# Foundations editorial artwork

Six original artworks generated with the built-in image_gen tool. Data and Intelligence reuse the two approved concepts. Workflow, Architecture, Value and Governance follow the same visual direction.

Production assets: `data.webp`, `intelligence.webp`, `workflow.webp`, `architecture.webp`, `value.webp`, `governance.webp`, each with `-640.webp` and `-1040.webp` responsive variants. Full images are 1672 by 941. Images are encoded as WebP at quality 84; resizing and encoding do not alter the artwork.

Feature artwork progressively adds subtle motion with a pause/play control. Reduced motion keeps the original static image; data saver defaults to paused; offscreen and hidden-tab animation is paused. Related article thumbnails remain static.

## Prompt set

Approved Data: prismatic glass-silk ribbons flowing through midnight navy space, cobalt, teal and violet with restrained amber highlights; a coherent current representing reliable data. No people, words, logos, circuits or literal diagrams.

Approved Intelligence: a recognizable brain sculpture made from flowing translucent dichroic glass, above a warm ivory museum plinth; cobalt, turquoise and amethyst with champagne refraction. No people, words, logos, circuits or medical illustration.
